# Quality impact rating — future idea

**Status:** outside v1.0, decided by the working group on
[2026-06-09](../../adr/2026-06-09-notes.md).

The group considered assigning an impact rating to quality findings, for example a numerical
scale of 1–10 or categories such as high, medium and low. No scale or representation was selected.

Keep this as a future idea. A later proposal would need to define what the rating measures,
who assigns it and how it relates to the existing quality results. It introduces no required
field or reporting obligation into v1.0 and has no committed delivery version.

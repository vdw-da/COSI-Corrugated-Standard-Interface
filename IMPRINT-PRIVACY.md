### Use of GitHub and sharing of content

We use the GitHub platform to provide, manage and collaborate on digital content.
For this purpose, selected repositories, files, documentation and project-related
information within our GitHub organisation may be made available to external users.
Personal data (such as GitHub usernames and email addresses) may be processed where
they are included in the shared content, arise through use of the platform or are
needed to grant access. Processing serves to provide and share the relevant content
and to collaborate with external users.

The platform provider is GitHub, Inc., USA. For details of how it handles your personal
data, please see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The company is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (DPF). The DPF is an
arrangement between the European Union and the United States intended to ensure
compliance with European data protection standards when data are processed in the
United States. Each DPF-certified company undertakes to comply with these standards.
Further information is available in the provider's
[DPF entry](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/s/participant-search/participant-detail?contact=true&id=a2zt0000000GnywAAC&status=Active).

### Inquiries by email, telephone or fax

If you contact us by email, telephone or fax, we store and process your inquiry,
including all personal data it contains (name, inquiry), to handle your request.
We do not disclose these data without your consent.

If your inquiry concerns documents or content provided through GitHub, it may be
forwarded to the responsible working groups where this is necessary to assess or
further process the inquiry. Inquiries that are not pursued further are deleted.

These data are processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your inquiry
relates to the performance of a contract or is necessary to take steps prior to
entering into a contract. In all other cases, processing is based on our legitimate
interest in effectively handling inquiries addressed to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR),
or on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) where requested; consent may be withdrawn
at any time. We retain data you send us through contact inquiries until you ask us
to delete them, withdraw your consent to their storage, or the purpose for storing
them no longer applies (for example, once your request has been dealt with).
Mandatory statutory provisions, particularly statutory retention periods, remain
unaffected.
